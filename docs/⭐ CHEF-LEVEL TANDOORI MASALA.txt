⭐ CHEF-LEVEL TANDOORI MASALA

	Quantity: ~200 g batch (store for 3 months easily)
	
	🌶 Ingredients:
		Jeera (cumin): 20 g
		Dhania seeds: 25 g
		Black pepper: 8 g
		Cloves (laung): 4 g
		Green cardamom: 4 g
		Cinnamon stick: 6 g
		Mace (javitri): 2 g
		Bay leaf: 2 g
		Kashmiri Red Chillies Whole: 15 g
		Dry Red Hot Chillies Whole(guntur): 10 g

		📌 Roasting Tip:
			Low flame par 45–50 seconds, jab halka aroma aaye, bas utar lo. Burn nahi hona chahiye.

	🧂 Powder spices:
		Garlic powder: 10 g
		Ginger powder: 10 g
		Turmeric: 6 g
		Dry mango powder (amchur): 8 g
		Red chilli powder Kashmiri: 15 g
		Smoked paprika (optional but BEST): 8 g
		Rock salt / sendha namak: 10 g
		Black salt: 5 g
		Kasuri methi (crushed): 8 g
		Sugar (color balancing): 5 g

	⭐ How to Make It – Step by Step:
		STEP 1 — Whole spices roast
			👉 Kadhai low flame par garam karo.
			👉 Saare whole masale daalo.
			👉 45–50 sec tak roast karo — bas aroma aana chahiye.
			👉 Thanda hone do.
		STEP 2 — Grind
			👉 Whole spices ko fine powder bana lo.
			👉 Agar mixer heat ho raha hai, 2–2 seconds ke pulse me grind karo.
		STEP 3 — Add powder spices
			👉 Ab is grind ki hui powder me ye sab milao: garlic, ginger, turmeric, paprika, amchur, salt etc.
		STEP 4 — Kasuri methi ko hand crush
			👉 Direct masale me sprinkle kar do.
		STEP 5 — Mix & store
			👉 Airtight glass jar me rakho.
			👉 Shelf life: 3–4 months
			👉 Smell stronger hota rahega time ke sath.

	🔥 WHY THIS MASALA IS PERFECT?
		✔ Commercial masalon se 10× deep flavor
		✔ Natural warm coffee-red color
		✔ Smoke-like earthy aroma
		✔ Cream, mayo, butter me instantly mix hota hai
		✔ Chicken shawarma ke liye unbeatable
		✔ Pita par lagane wale sauces me game changer

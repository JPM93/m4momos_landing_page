👑 Royal Sultan Tangdi Shawarma (Final v1.0)

Quantity:
	12 Shawarmas

Chicken:
	12 Chicken Leg (Tangdi)

Recommended Weight:
	1 Leg = 180–220 g

Use:
	Skinless Chicken Leg

🎯 Flavor Profile:
	Super Juicy
	Creamy
	Buttery
	Light Smoky
	Royal Richness
	Very Mild Tang
	Garlic Rich
	Restaurant Premium

STEP 1 – Chicken Preparation
	Method:
		Remove skin completely.
		Remove lower cartilage.
		Keep bone inside.
		Deep score: 3 Cuts
		Depth: 1.5–2 cm

	📌 NOTE:
		Deep cuts allow the marinade to penetrate till the bone.

STEP 2 – First Marination
	🥄 Ingredients:
		Lemon Juice: 15 g
		Ginger Garlic Paste: 40 g
		Salt: 14 g
		White Pepper: 2 g

	Method:
		Apply evenly on every tangdi.

	Rest:
		30 Minutes in Refrigerated

STEP 3 – Royal Cream Marination
	🥄 Cream Base
		Thick Hung Curd: 170 g
		Mayonnaise: 80 g
		Fresh Cream: 60 g
		Full Fat Milk: 20 g

	🥄 Fat
		Unsalted Butter: 30 g
		Smoked Mustard Oil: 20 g

	🥄 Flavor
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 28 g
		Kashmiri Chilli: 6 g
		White Pepper: 2 g
		Garlic Paste: 18 g
		Garlic Powder: 3 g
		Roasted Cumin Powder: 2 g
		Sugar: 5 g
		MSG: 2 g

	🥄 Moisture Lock
		Milk Powder: 18 g
		Corn Flour: 5 g

	🥄 Secret Richness
		Cashew Paste: 30 g
		Cheddar Cheese Powder: 6 g

	🥄 Mild Tang
		Lemon Juice: 8 g

	📌 NOTE:
		Do NOT use Rice Flour.
		Do NOT increase lemon.

STEP 4 – Marination Time
	Minimum: 12 Hours
	Recommended: 18 Hours
	Best: 24 Hours
	Temperature: 0–4°C

STEP 5 – Air Fryer SOP (Juicy Tangdi)
	Preheat:
        180°C for 3 Minutes
	
    First Cook:
        180°C for 10 Minutes
	
    Flip & Brush Melted Butter:
	
    Second Cook:
        180°C for 8 Minutes

	Check Internal Temperature:
        Near Bone Target: 74–76°C

	Rest:
		5 Minutes

STEP 6 – Gas Finish
	Direct Flame 10 Seconds Each Side
	Brush 15 g Unsalted Butter

STEP 7 – Coal Smoke
	Charcoal: 25 g
	Ghee: 4 g
	Time: 3 Minutes

STEP 8 – Royal Chicken Glaze
	🥄 Ingredients
		Concentrated Chicken Stock: 35 g
		Unsalted Butter: 15 g
		Chicken Fat: 8 g
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 2 g
		MSG: 0.5 g
		Honey: 3 g

	Method:
		Heat stock.
		Add butter.
		Add chicken fat.
		Add masala.
		Add honey.
		Coat hot tangdi.

		Rest for 2 Minutes

⭐ AIR FRYER JUICINESS RULES
	✔ Always use Chicken Leg.
	✔ Never overcook.
	✔ Rest before flame.
	✔ Rest after flame.
	✔ Chicken Stock Glaze is mandatory.
	✔ Never cook glaze.
	✔ Coat only.

⭐ Final Result
	✔ Outside Beautiful Char
	✔ Inside Extremely Juicy
	✔ Aroma Butter, Garlic, Cream, Smoke
	✔ Texture Fall-off-the-bone Tender
	✔ Restaurant Level ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

👑 🥣 Royal Sultan Mayo (Final v1.0)

Quantity:
	~450 g
	(12 × 30 g + Premium Buffer)

🎯 Flavor Profile:

	Ultra Creamy
	Royal Rich
	Cashew Creamy
	Buttery
	Mild Garlic
	Light Sweetness
	Almost Zero Tang
	Silky
	Restaurant Premium
	Dip Worthy

🥄 Premium Base
	Thick Premium Mayonnaise: 240 g
	Fresh Cream: 60 g
	Hung Curd (Very Thick): 15 g
	Full Fat Milk: 20 g

🥄 Premium Fat
	Unsalted Butter: 20 g
	Neutral Oil: 8 g

🥄 Royal Cream Base
	Cashew Paste: 35 g
	Cheddar Cheese Powder: 5 g

🥄 Aroma
	Roasted Garlic Paste: 8 g
	Garlic Powder: 1 g
	White Pepper: 1 g
	Roasted Cumin Powder: 1 g

🥄 Sweet Balance
	Honey: 6 g
	Sugar: 3 g

🥄 Seasoning
	Salt: 2 g
	MSG: 1 g

🥄 Colour
	Burnt Onion Paste: 10 g
	Paprika: 1 g

🥄 Mild Freshness
	Lemon Juice: 2 g
	White Vinegar: ❌ NOT REQUIRED

⭐ Secret Ingredient 1
	Cashew Cream
	Cashew: 35 g
	Hot Water: 60 ml

	Method:
		Soak 30 Minutes Blend
		Until completely smooth.
		Add complete paste.

⭐ Secret Ingredient 2
	Burnt Onion Paste
	Onion 100–120 g

	Method:
		Roast directly over flame.
		Remove burnt layer.
		Blend soft onion.
		Use only 10 g

⭐ Secret Ingredient 3(Optional)

	Chicken Stock: 5 g

	📌 NOTE
		Only if sauce is used exclusively for chicken products.
		If fries, sandwiches, or vegetarian items ke saath serve karna hai to skip this ingredient.

⭐ Secret Ingredient 4
	NO COAL SMOKE

	📌 NOTE
		This sauce should remain Clean Creamy, Royal and Smoke free.

🥣 Mixing Method

	STEP 1: Whisk together
		Mayonnaise, Fresh Cream, Hung Curd & Milk Until silky.

	STEP 2: Add and Whisk
		Cashew Paste, Burnt Onion Paste & Roasted Garlic Paste for 2 Minutes.

	STEP 3: Add and Wisk
		Cheddar Cheese Powder, Garlic Powder, White Pepper, Roasted Cumin, Salt, Sugar, MSG & Paprika Whisk Until smooth.

	STEP 4: Add and Wisk
		Unsalted Butter, Neutral Oil, Honey, Lemon Juice and Whisk for 3 Minutes.

	STEP 5: Optional
		Add Chicken Stock & Whisk gently.

	STEP 6
		Cover & Refrigerate.
		Minimum: 6 Hours
		Best: Overnight (10–12 Hours)

📌 Storage
	👉 Temperature 0–4°C
	👉 Shelf Life 3 Days
	👉 Always use Clean Dry Spoon.

⭐ Chef's Secret Tips
	✔ Premium Mayo only.
	✔ Use Unsalted Butter only.
	✔ Butter should be lukewarm.
	✔ Cashew paste must be completely smooth.
	✔ Hung curd should be water-free.
	✔ Do NOT increase garlic.
	✔ Do NOT increase lemon.
	✔ This sauce should never taste tangy.
	✔ Overnight resting gives the best flavour.

🥗 Premium Veg Strategy – Royal Sultan Tangdi Shawarma (Final v1.0)

Quantity: Per Shawarma

🎯 Flavor Profile:

	Fresh
	Crispy
	Cooling
	Mild Sweet
	Very Mild Tang
	Restaurant Premium
	Supports Royal Sultan Mayo
	Does NOT Overpower Chicken

🥄 Ingredients

	Iceberg Lettuce (Thin Shredded): 18 g
	Purple Cabbage (Fine Julienne): 8 g
	Pickled Onion (Lightly Drained): 6 g
	Cucumber Julienne (Seed Removed): 8 g
	Total Veg: 40 g

⭐ Iceberg Lettuce

	Method
		Wash thoroughly.
		Soak in Ice Water for 10 Minutes & Dry completely.
		Thin shred.
		Store chilled.

	📌 NOTE
		Lettuce should remain Cold, Crispy and Dry

⭐ Purple Cabbage

	Method
		Very fine julienne.
		Soak in ice water for 5 Minutes & Dry properly.

	📌 NOTE
		Adds Crunch, Natural Sweetness, Premium Appearance Without adding tang.

⭐ Pickled Onion

	Quantity Only 6 g

	Method
		Use Restaurant Style Pickled Onion.
		Drain excess liquid completely.

	📌 NOTE
		Use very little.
		This shawarma should NOT taste tangy.
		Onion should only give a light sweet-pickled note.

⭐ Cucumber

	Method:
		Remove soft seed centre if watery.
		Cut into thin julienne.
		Pat dry using paper towel.

	📌 NOTE
		Gives freshness and cooling bite.
		Do not use wet cucumber.

⭐ Optional Premium Garnish

	Finely Chopped Fresh Parsley: 0.5–1 g

	📌 NOTE
		Very lightly sprinkle just before wrapping.
		Do NOT use coriander.

⭐ Veg Layer Order
	1. Iceberg Lettuce
	2. Purple Cabbage
	3. Cucumber Julienne
	4. Pickled Onion
	5. Light Parsley Sprinkle (Optional)

📌 Storage SOP
	👉 Store every vegetable in a separate container.
	👉 Do NOT premix.
	👉 Keep refrigerated at 0–4°C.
	👉 Drain moisture regularly.

❌ Do NOT Add
	Tomato, Green Capsicum, Jalapeño, Carrot, Fresh Coriander, Mint, Extra Pickled Onion, Heavy Oregano Seasoning

	📌 REASON
		These ingredients will overpower the Royal Sultan Mayo
				+
		Juicy Tangdi Chicken and shift the flavour away from the intended royal, creamy profile.

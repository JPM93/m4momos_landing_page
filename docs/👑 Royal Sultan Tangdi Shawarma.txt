STEP 1 – Chicken Preparation
	Method:
		Remove skin completely.
		Remove lower cartilage.
		Keep bone inside.
		Deep score: 3 Cuts
		Depth: 1.5–2 cm

	📌 NOTE:
		Deep cuts allow the marinade to penetrate till the bone.

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Lemon Juice: 15 g
		Ginger Garlic Paste: 40 g
		Salt: 14 g
		White Pepper: 2 g

	Method:
		Apply evenly on every tangdi.

	Rest:
		30 Minutes in Refrigerated

STEP 3 – Royal Cream Marination
	🥄 Cream Base
		Thick Hung Curd: 170 g
		Mayonnaise: 80 g
		Fresh Cream: 60 g
		Full Fat Milk: 20 g

	🥄 Fat
		Unsalted Butter: 30 g
		Smoked Mustard Oil: 20 g

	🥄 Flavor
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 28 g
		Kashmiri Chilli: 6 g
		White Pepper: 2 g
		Garlic Paste: 18 g
		Garlic Powder: 3 g
		Roasted Cumin Powder: 2 g
		Sugar: 5 g
		MSG: 2 g

	🥄 Moisture Lock
		Milk Powder: 18 g
		Corn Flour: 5 g

	🥄 Secret Richness
		Cashew Paste: 30 g
		Cheddar Cheese Powder: 6 g

	🥄 Mild Tang
		Lemon Juice: 8 g

	📌 NOTE:
		Do NOT use Rice Flour.
		Do NOT increase lemon.

STEP 4 – Marination Time
	Minimum: 12 Hours
	Recommended: 18 Hours
	Best: 24 Hours
	Temperature: 0–4°C

STEP 5 – Air Fryer SOP (Juicy Tangdi)
	Preheat:
        180°C for 3 Minutes
	
    First Cook:
        180°C for 10 Minutes
	
    Flip & Brush Melted Butter:
	
    Second Cook:
        180°C for 8 Minutes

	Check Internal Temperature:
        Near Bone Target: 74–76°C

	Rest:
		5 Minutes

STEP 6 – Gas Finish
	Direct Flame 10 Seconds Each Side
	Brush 15 g Unsalted Butter

STEP 7 – Coal Smoke
	Charcoal: 25 g
	Ghee: 4 g
	Time: 3 Minutes

STEP 8 – Royal Chicken Glaze
	🥄 Ingredients
		Concentrated Chicken Stock: 35 g
		Unsalted Butter: 15 g
		Chicken Fat: 8 g

👑 🥣 Royal Sultan Mayo (Final v1.0)

Quantity:
	~450 g
	(12 × 30 g + Premium Buffer)

🎯 Flavor Profile:

	Ultra Creamy
	Royal Rich
	Cashew Creamy
	Buttery
	Mild Garlic
	Light Sweetness
	Almost Zero Tang
	Silky
	Restaurant Premium
	Dip Worthy

🥄 Premium Base
	Thick Premium Mayonnaise: 240 g
	Fresh Cream: 60 g
	Hung Curd (Very Thick): 15 g
	Full Fat Milk: 20 g

🥄 Premium Fat
	Unsalted Butter: 20 g
	Neutral Oil: 8 g

🥄 Royal Cream Base
	Cashew Paste: 35 g
	Cheddar Cheese Powder: 5 g

🥄 Aroma
	Roasted Garlic Paste: 8 g
	Garlic Powder: 1 g
	White Pepper: 1 g
	Roasted Cumin Powder: 1 g

🥄 Sweet Balance
	Honey: 6 g
	Sugar: 3 g

🥄 Seasoning
	Salt: 2 g
	MSG: 1 g

🥄 Colour
	Burnt Onion Paste: 10 g
	Paprika: 1 g

🥄 Mild Freshness
	Lemon Juice: 2 g
	White Vinegar: ❌ NOT REQUIRED

⭐ Secret Ingredient 1
	Cashew Cream
	Cashew: 35 g
	Hot Water: 60 ml

	Method:
		Soak 30 Minutes Blend
		Until completely smooth.
		Add complete paste.

⭐ Secret Ingredient 2
	Burnt Onion Paste
	Onion 100–120 g

	Method:
		Roast directly over flame.
		Remove burnt layer.
		Blend soft onion.
		Use only 10 g

⭐ Secret Ingredient 3(Optional)

	Chicken Stock: 5 g

	📌 NOTE
		Only if sauce is used exclusively for chicken products.
		If fries, sandwiches, or vegetarian items ke saath serve karna hai to skip this ingredient.

⭐ Secret Ingredient 4
	NO COAL SMOKE

	📌 NOTE
		This sauce should remain Clean Creamy, Royal and Smoke free.

🥣 Mixing Method

	STEP 1: Whisk together
		Mayonnaise, Fresh Cream, Hung Curd & Milk Until silky.

	STEP 2: Add and Whisk
		Cashew Paste, Burnt Onion Paste & Roasted Garlic Paste for 2 Minutes.

	STEP 3: Add and Wisk
		Cheddar Cheese Powder, Garlic Powder, White Pepper, Roasted Cumin, Salt, Sugar, MSG & Paprika Whisk Until smooth.

	STEP 4: Add and Wisk
		Unsalted Butter, Neutral Oil, Honey, Lemon Juice and Whisk for 3 Minutes.

	STEP 5: Optional
		Add Chicken Stock & Whisk gently.

	STEP 6
		Cover & Refrigerate.
		Minimum: 6 Hours
		Best: Overnight (10–12 Hours)

📌 Storage
	👉 Temperature 0–4°C
	👉 Shelf Life 3 Days
	👉 Always use Clean Dry Spoon.

⭐ Chef's Secret Tips
	✔ Premium Mayo only.
	✔ Use Unsalted Butter only.
	✔ Butter should be lukewarm.
	✔ Cashew paste must be completely smooth.
	✔ Hung curd should be water-free.
	✔ Do NOT increase garlic.
	✔ Do NOT increase lemon.
	✔ This sauce should never taste tangy.
	✔ Overnight resting gives the best flavour.

	Method
		Wash thoroughly.
		Soak in Ice Water for 10 Minutes & Dry completely.
		Thin shred.
		Store chilled.

		Lettuce should remain Cold, Crispy and Dry

⭐ Purple Cabbage

	Method
		Very fine julienne.
		Soak in ice water for 5 Minutes & Dry properly.

	📌 NOTE
		Adds Crunch, Natural Sweetness, Premium Appearance Without adding tang.

⭐ Pickled Onion

	Quantity Only 6 g

	Method
		Use Restaurant Style Pickled Onion.
		Drain excess liquid completely.

	📌 NOTE
		Use very little.
		This shawarma should NOT taste tangy.
		Onion should only give a light sweet-pickled note.

⭐ Cucumber

	Method:
		Remove soft seed centre if watery.
		Cut into thin julienne.
		Pat dry using paper towel.

	📌 NOTE
		Gives freshness and cooling bite.
		Do not use wet cucumber.

⭐ Optional Premium Garnish

	Finely Chopped Fresh Parsley: 0.5–1 g

	📌 NOTE
		Very lightly sprinkle just before wrapping.
		Do NOT use coriander.

🔥 Air Fryer Juicy Tangdi SOP (Final v1.0)

🎯 Goal

	Maximum Juiciness, Light Char, Minimum Moisture Loss & Restaurant Consistency

🥩 Chicken Selection

	Preferred: Skinless Chicken Leg
	Weight: 180–220 g Each
	Use: Deboned Butterfly Cut
	Thickness: Approximately 2–2.5 cm

🧂 Marination

	Minimum: 12 Hours
	Recommended: 18–24 Hours
	Always Refrigerated: 0–4°C
	Before Cooking: Remove chicken from refrigerator 15–20 minutes earlier.

	📌 NOTE
		Do not cook ice-cold chicken directly.

🔥 Air Fryer Preheat

	Temperature: 180°C
	Time: 3 Minutes

🥄 Basket Setup

	Lightly brush or spray the basket with neutral oil.
	Arrange chicken in a single layer.
	Leave space between pieces.
	Do NOT stack.

🔥 First Cook

	Temperature: 180°C
	Time: 8 Minutes

🔄 Flip

	Flip gently.

	Brush lightly with:

		Unsalted Butter
			OR
		Neutral Oil

	Use only a thin coating.

🔥 Second Cook

	Temperature: 180°C
	Time: 6–8 Minutes

	Check the thickest part near where the bone was removed.
	Cook until the chicken is safely cooked through; adjust the time if needed based on thickness.

⏳ Rest

	Transfer chicken to a wire rack.

	Rest: 5 Minutes

	📌 NOTE
		Do NOT cut immediately.
		Resting helps retain juices.

🔥 Quick Flame Finish

	Direct Flame: 8–10 Seconds Each Side
	Purpose: Colour, Char & Aroma

	📌 NOT Further Cooking

🧈 Butter Brush

	Brush 10g Unsalted Butter

	📌 Immediately after flame.

🌫 Coal Smoke

	Charcoal: 25 g
	Ghee: 4 g
	Smoke: 3 Minutes
	Rest: 5 Minutes

✨ Royal Chicken Glaze

	Heat together:
		Chicken Stock: 35 g
		Unsalted Butter: 15 g
		Rendered Chicken Fat: 8 g (Optional)
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 2 g
		MSG: 0.5 g

	Remove from heat & Add 3g Honey
	Brush generously over the cooked tangdi.

	📌 NOTE:
		If using lemon juice, add it after removing the glaze from the heat.

⭐ Juiciness Rules

	✔ Chicken must not be overcrowded.
	✔ Never cook straight from the fridge.
	✔ Always use a wire rack for resting.
	✔ Never skip the resting stage.
	✔ Butter brushing should be light.
	✔ Flame finish should be quick.
	✔ Glaze after cooking, never before.
	✔ Slice only while assembling the shawarma.

🚫 Common Mistakes

	❌ Overcooking
	❌ High temperature throughout
	❌ Cutting immediately
	❌ Thick layer of butter
	❌ Long flame exposure
	❌ Skipping chicken glaze
	❌ Overcrowding the basket

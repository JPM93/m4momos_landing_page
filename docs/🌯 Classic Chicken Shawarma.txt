🌯 Classic Chicken Shawarma

	Quantity: 12 shawarmas
	Chicken Quantity: 1.2 kg (12 × 100 g)

	STEP 1 – First Marination:
		Ingredients:
			Lemon Juice: 30 g
			Ginger Garlic Paste: 40 g
			Salt: 16 g
		NOTE: Mix karke 20–30 min rest.

	STEP 2 – Main Shawarma Marination:
		Ingredients:
			Thick Hung Curd: 150 g
			Mayonnaise: 110 g
			Melted Butter: 35 g
			Mustard Oil (smoked & cooled): 30 g
			Chef Tandoori Masala: 48 g
			Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 8 g
			Smoked Paprika: 5 g
			Coriander Powder: 8 g
			Cumin Powder: 5 g
			White Pepper: 3 g
			MSG (Ajinomoto): 2 g
			Lemon Juice: 20 g
			Sugar: 5 g
			Milk Powder: 15 g
			Corn Flour: 8 g
			Rice Flour: 4 g
			Garlic Paste: 15 g 

		NOTE: Marinate minimum 8 hours, preferably overnight (12–18 hours).

	STEP 3 - Air Fryer Cooking:
		Temperature: 180°C
		Time: Flip after 8/m and cook for more 7/m, rest 2 min and after Butter Brus & 190°C for 1/m
		Internal Chicken Temperature: 74-76°C

	STEP 4 - Gas Finish:
		Direct flame for 20-30 seconds each sides.
		After flame brush 20g butter.

	STEP 5 - Coal Smoke(dum):
		Ingredients:
			Coal: Food-grade charcoal
			25-30 g(1 medium piece)
			Ghee: 4 g
		NOTE: 4-5/minutes maximum, and after coal 5 minutes rest

	STEP 6 - Final Shawarma Chicken Seasoning:
		Ingredients:
			Chicken Stock: 20 g
			Butter: 15 g
			Lemon: 5 g
			Checf Tandoori Masala: 3 g
			MSG: 0.5 g
			
		NOTE: After chicken slices.

🧄 Signature Arabic Garlic Sauce

	Quantity: ~190 g (12 × 15 g + handling buffer)

	Ingredients:
		Thick Mayonnaise: 105 g
		Hung Curd (Thick Greek Yogurt): 40 g
		Roasted Garlic (very fine paste): 14 g
		Rice Bran Oil: 12 g
		Lemon Juice: 8 g
		White Vinegar: 2 g
		White Pepper: 1 g
		Salt: 2 g
		Sugar: 1.5 g
		MSG: 0.5 g
		Butter Essence: 1 Drop

	Method:
		1 Mayo aur hung curd ko smooth whisk karo.
		2 Fresh garlic paste add karo.
		3 Oil dheere-dheere add karte hue whisk karo.
		4 Lemon juice aur vinegar add karo.
		5 Salt, sugar, white pepper (aur optional MSG) mix karo.
		6 2–3 minute whisk karo.
		7 Fridge me 3–4 ghante rest do.

	Shelf Life: 3-4/days @0–4°C in refrigerator

🤍 Signature Creamy Arabic White Sauce

	Quantity: ~190 g (12 × 15 g + handling buffer)

	Ingredients:
		Thick Mayonnaise: 95 g
		Hung Curd: 45 g
		Fresh Cream: 18 g
		Rice Bran Oil: 8 g
		Lemon Juice: 6 g
		White Vinegar: 2 g
		Garlic Powder: 1 g
		White Pepper: 1 g
		Honey: 2 g
		Salt: 1.5 g

	Method:
		Mayo, hung curd aur cream ko smooth whisk karo.
		Rice bran oil add karo.
		Lemon aur vinegar mix karo.
		Garlic powder, white pepper, honey aur salt add karo.
		2 minute whisk karo.
		Fridge me 2–3 ghante rest do.

	Shelf Life: 3-4/days @0–4°C in refrigerator

🥣 Premium Afghani Creamy Mayo (Final v1.0)

Quantity:
	~400 g
	(30 g × 12 = 360 g + Handling Buffer)

🎨 Flavor Profile:

	Extra Creamy

	Royal Richness

	Cashew Creamy

	Light Coffee Cream Color

	Mild Garlic

	Very Mild Tang

	Buttery Finish

	Restaurant Style

	Dip Worthy

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Creamy Base Ingredients:

	Thick Premium Mayonnaise: 230 g

	Fresh Cream: 55 g

	Hung Curd (Thick): 15 g

	Full Fat Milk: 20 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Richness Ingredients:

	Unsalted Butter
	(Melted & Cooled): 18 g

	Neutral Oil
	(Sunflower / Soybean): 8 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Premium Ingredients:

	Cashew Nuts: 20 g

	Cheddar Cheese Powder: 4 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Flavor Ingredients:

	Roasted Garlic Paste: 8 g

	White Pepper Powder: 1 g

	Roasted Cumin Powder: 1.5 g

	Salt: 2 g

	Sugar: 4 g

	Honey: 6 g

	MSG: 1 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Color Ingredients:

	Burnt Onion Paste: 12 g

	Smoked Paprika: 2 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 Mild Tang:

	Lemon Juice: 4 g

	White Vinegar: 1 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Secret Ingredient 1 – Cashew Cream

	Cashew: 20 g

	Hot Water: 50 ml

	Method:

		Soak Cashew

		30 Minutes

		Blend with
		50 ml hot water

		Until completely smooth.

		Use complete paste.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Secret Ingredient 2 – Burnt Onion Paste

	Medium Onion:
		100–120 g

	Method:

		Roast directly over flame.

		Remove burnt outer layer.

		Blend soft onion.

		Use only 12 g.

	📌 NOTE:

		Gives natural
		Light Coffee Cream Colour.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Secret Ingredient 3 – Coal Smoke

	Charcoal:
		20–25 g

	Ghee:
		4 g

	Method:

		Give smoke

		Maximum

		45–60 Seconds.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥣 Mixing Method

	STEP 1

		Whisk together:

			Mayonnaise

			Fresh Cream

			Hung Curd

			Milk

	-------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 2

		Add:

			Cashew Cream

			Burnt Onion Paste

			Roasted Garlic Paste

		Whisk.

	-------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 3

		Add:

			Cheese Powder

			White Pepper

			Roasted Cumin

			Salt

			Sugar

			MSG

			Smoked Paprika

		Mix until smooth.

	-------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 4

		Add:

			Melted Butter

			Neutral Oil

			Honey

			Lemon Juice

			Vinegar

		Whisk

		3 Minutes.

	-------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 5

		Give Coal Smoke

		45–60 Seconds.

	-------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 6

		Cover

		Refrigerate

		Minimum

		4 Hours

		Best

		Overnight.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

📌 Storage

	Temperature:

		0–4°C

	Shelf Life:

		3 Days

	Always use
	Clean Dry Spoon.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Chef's Secret Tips

	✔ Use Premium Thick Mayo.
	✔ Cashew Paste should be completely smooth.
	✔ Butter should be lukewarm, not hot.
	✔ Don't increase garlic.
	✔ Tang should remain very low.
	✔ Rest overnight for maximum flavour.
	✔ Burnt Onion should give only colour, not burnt taste.

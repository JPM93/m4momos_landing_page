	Black Peppercorns: 7 g

	Mace (Javitri): 1.5 g

	Bay Leaf: 2 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🧂 Powder Spices:

	Garlic Powder: 12 g

	Ginger Powder: 8 g

	Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 15 g

	Smoked Paprika: 8 g

	Turmeric Powder: 4 g

	Dry Mango Powder (Amchur): 5 g

	Kasuri Methi: 7 g

	Sugar: 4 g

	Rock Salt / Sendha Namak: 6 g

	Black Salt: 2 g

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Why Salt Is Reduced:

	Previous Version:

		Rock Salt: 10 g
		Black Salt: 5 g

	Final Version:

		Rock Salt: 6 g
		Black Salt: 2 g

	📌 NOTE:

		Classic Chicken Shawarma ki
		First Marination me already
		Salt separately add hota hai.

		MSG bhi marinade aur glaze
		me separately controlled hai.

		Isliye Tandoori Masala ko
		high-salt seasoning nahi banana.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Why Amchur Is Reduced:

	Previous:
		8 g

	Final:
		5 g

	📌 REASON:

		Classic Chicken recipe me already:

			Lemon Juice – First Marination
			Lemon Juice – Main Marination
			Lemon Juice – Final Glaze
			Pickled Onion
			Garlic Sauce

		present hain.

		Amchur ko background tang
		ke liye use karna hai.

		Masala ko achari ya overly
		tangy direction me nahi le jana.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Why Guntur Chilli Is Reduced:

	Previous:
		10 g

	Final:
		6 g

	📌 REASON:

		Tandoori aroma aur flavour
		heat se zyada important hai.

		Classic Shawarma ko spicy
		banana goal nahi hai.

		Sauces aur chicken ka flavour
		clearly taste hona chahiye.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🔥 STEP 1 – Whole Spice Roasting:

	Add:

		Coriander Seeds
		Cumin Seeds
		Black Pepper
		Cloves
		Green Cardamom
		Cinnamon
		Mace
		Bay Leaf
		Kashmiri Whole Chillies
		Guntur Whole Chillies

	Method:

		Heavy-bottom pan ko
		low flame par heat karo.

		Whole spices add karo.

		Gently roast while stirring.

		Approx.:
			45–60 Seconds

		Jaise hi fresh aroma
		release hone lage:

			Heat OFF.

	📌 IMPORTANT:

		Spices ko dark brown
		ya burnt mat hone do.

		Over-roasting se masala
		bitter ho sakta hai.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

❄️ STEP 2 – Cool Completely:

	Roasted spices ko plate
	par spread karo.

	Completely room temperature
	tak cool hone do.

	📌 NOTE:

		Warm spices grind karne se
		essential oils unnecessarily
		heat ho sakte hain.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⚙️ STEP 3 – Grinding:

	Cooled whole spices ko
	grinder me add karo.

	Short pulses me grind karo.

	Target:

		Fine Powder

	📌 NOTE:

		Grinder jar unnecessarily
		hot nahi hona chahiye.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🥄 STEP 4 – Add Powder Spices:

	Ground whole-spice mixture me add:

		Garlic Powder
		Ginger Powder
		Kashmiri Chilli Powder
		Smoked Paprika
		Turmeric
		Amchur
		Sugar
		Rock Salt
		Black Salt

	Mix thoroughly.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🌿 STEP 5 – Kasuri Methi:

	Kasuri Methi:
		7 g

	Leaves ko palms ke beech
	gently crush karo.

	Masala me add karo.

	Again mix thoroughly.

	📌 NOTE:

		Kasuri Methi ko powder
		jaisa over-grind mat karo.

		Lightly crushed leaves better
		tandoori aroma provide karti hain.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ STEP 6 – Final Blend:

	Complete mixture ko:

		20–30 Seconds

	low-speed / pulse blend do.

	Goal:

		Uniform Spice Distribution

		Not Excessive Grinding

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

🧪 STEP 7 – Rest Before First Use:

	Freshly prepared masala ko
	airtight container me transfer karo.

	Before First Use:

		12–24 Hours Rest

	📌 NOTE:

		Is resting period me individual
		spice aromas settle aur integrate
		hote hain.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

📦 Storage:

	Container:
		Airtight Glass Jar

	Storage:
		Cool
		Dry
		Dark Place

	Avoid:
		Direct Sunlight
		Heat
		Steam
		Wet Spoon

	Best Aroma:
		Preferably use within 6–8 Weeks

		Quality gradually decline hogi.

		Background Acidity

		Controlled Savoury Depth

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

⭐ Important Balance Rules:

	✔ Do NOT increase Garam Spices.

	✔ Do NOT increase Black Salt.

	✔ Do NOT increase Amchur unnecessarily.

	✔ Do NOT make Guntur Chilli dominant.

	✔ Smoked Paprika should support
	  smoke, not dominate it.

	✔ Coal Smoke + Gas Char will provide
	  additional smoky character during cooking.

	✔ Masala should enhance chicken,
	  not hide chicken flavour.

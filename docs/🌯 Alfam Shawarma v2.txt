🌯 Alfam Shawarma (Final v3.0)

	Quantity: 12 Shawarmas
	Chicken Quantity: 1.2 kg (12 × 100 g)

	STEP 1 – First Marination:

		🥄 Ingredients:
			Lemon Juice: 28 g
			Salt: 16 g
			Ginger Garlic Paste: 45 g
			Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 8 g

		📌 NOTE:
			Mix all ingredients thoroughly with chicken.
			Rest for 20–30 minutes in refrigerator.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 2 – Main Alfam Shawarma Marination:

		🥄 Dairy & Fat Ingredients:
			Hung Curd: 120 g
			Mayonnaise: 100 g
			Fresh Cream: 60 g
			Melted Butter: 30 g
			Mustard Oil: 35 g

		🥄 Moisture Lock Ingredients:
			Milk Powder: 20 g
			Corn Flour: 10 g
			Rice Flour: 5 g

		🥄 Alfam Spice Base Ingredients:
			Dana Masala / Resham Patti: 16 g
			Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 12 g
			Paprika: 8 g
			Coriander Powder: 8 g
			Cumin Powder: 5 g
			Black Pepper Powder: 3 g
			White Pepper Powder: 2 g
			Kasuri Methi: 2 g
			Turmeric Powder: 1.5 g
			Black Salt: 2 g
			MSG: 2 g
			Baking Soda: 1 g

		🥄 Aroma Boost Ingredients:
			Ginger Garlic Paste: 20 g
			Lemon Juice: 15 g

		📌 NOTE:
			Add Step-1 marinated chicken into this mixture.
			Coat every piece evenly.
			Marinate minimum 12 hours.
			Best Result: 12–18 hours in refrigerator.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 3 – Air Fryer Cooking:

		Temperature:
			180°C

		Cooking:
			Cook for 8 minutes.

		Flip Chicken.

		Cook Again:
			7 minutes.

		Rest:
			2 minutes.

		Brush:
			Light coating of melted butter.

		Final Color:
			190°C for 1 minute.

		Internal Chicken Temperature:
			74–76°C

		📌 NOTE:
			Do not overcook.
			Remove chicken immediately after reaching temperature.
			Avoid keeping chicken inside hot air fryer after cooking.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 4 – Gas Finish:

		Direct Flame:
			20–30 seconds on each side.

		Brush:
			20 g Melted Butter

		📌 NOTE:
			This gives authentic Alfam aroma and charred finish.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 5 – Coal Smoke (Dum):

		🥄 Ingredients:
			Food Grade Charcoal:
				25–30 g (1 Medium Piece)

			Ghee:
				5 g

		📌 NOTE:
			Give smoke for only 4–5 minutes.
			Rest another 5 minutes before slicing.
			Do not exceed smoke time or bitterness may develop.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 6 – Restaurant Secret Chicken Glaze (After Slicing)

		🥄 Ingredients:
			Concentrated Chicken Stock: 20 g
			Melted Butter: 15 g
			Lemon Juice: 4 g
			Alfam Masala: 3 g
			MSG: 0.5 g

		Method:
			Slice the chicken.
			Heat a pan on high flame.
			Add Chicken Stock and Butter.
			Add Alfam Masala and MSG.
			Add sliced chicken.
			Toss for 30–40 seconds.
			Finish with Lemon Juice.

		📌 NOTE:
			This is the restaurant secret that gives
			glossy,
			juicy,
			buttery chicken coating.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Chef's Secret Tips

		✔ Use boneless chicken thigh instead of breast whenever possible.
		  Thigh meat remains naturally juicier.

		✔ Chicken pieces should be around 2–2.5 cm thick.
		  Very small pieces dry out quickly.

		✔ Always rest chicken for 5 minutes after coal smoke before slicing.

		✔ Never slice immediately after cooking.
		  Resting allows juices to redistribute.

		✔ Toss sliced chicken with Chicken Glaze only when preparing shawarma.
		  Do not glaze the whole batch in advance.

		✔ Hold glazed chicken at 60–65°C during service for maximum juiciness.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Final Commercial Result

		Flavor:
			Rich Alfam Style

		Texture:
			Extremely Juicy

		Color:
			Restaurant Style Smoky Red

		Aroma:
			Smoky + Buttery + Charred

		Mouthfeel:
			Soft, Moist & Succulent

		Perfect For:
			Air Fryer Commercial Shawarma
			Premium Restaurant Style Alfam Shawarma

🔥 Signature Creamy Smoky Shawarma Mayo (Final v3.0)

	Quantity: ~400 g
	(30 g × 12 = 360 g + Handling Buffer)

	🎨 Flavor Profile:
		Extra Creamy
		Rich Garlic
		Buttery Finish
		Light Smoky
		Mild Sweetness
		Very Mild Tang
		Restaurant Style Dip Mayo
		Perfect Companion for Alfam Shawarma

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Base Ingredients:

		Thick Mayonnaise: 250 g

		Fresh Cream: 45 g

		Hung Curd (Thick): 20 g

		Full Fat Milk: 20 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Fat Ingredients:

		Melted Unsalted Butter: 18 g

		Neutral Oil (Sunflower / Soybean): 8 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Flavor Ingredients:

		Roasted Garlic Paste: 10 g

		Garlic Powder: 4 g

		Roasted Cumin Powder: 2 g

		Smoked Paprika: 3 g

		White Pepper Powder: 2 g

		Black Pepper Powder: 1 g

		Salt: 2 g

		Sugar: 4 g

		Honey: 5 g

		MSG: 1.5 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Color Ingredients:

		Burnt Onion Paste: 10 g

		Paprika: 2 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Mild Tang Ingredients:

		Lemon Juice: 6 g

		White Vinegar: 1 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Secret Ingredient 1 – Cheese Powder

		Cheddar Cheese Powder: 4 g

		📌 NOTE:
			Gives restaurant-style creamy umami flavour.
			Makes customers want extra mayo for dipping.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Secret Ingredient 2 – Butter Flavor

		Butter Flavor Powder: 1 g

			OR

		Food Grade Butter Essence:
			1–2 Drops

		📌 NOTE:
			Optional but highly recommended for premium restaurant finish.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Secret Ingredient 3 – Burnt Onion Paste

		Medium Onion:
			100–120 g (Raw)

		Method:

			Cut onion into two halves.

			Roast directly over flame for
			2–3 minutes while rotating.

			Remove burnt outer layer.

			Blend soft roasted onion into
			a smooth paste.

			Use only 10 g in sauce.

		📌 NOTE:
			This provides natural light coffee-brown colour
			and roasted sweetness without giving achari flavour.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Secret Ingredient 4 – Coal Smoke

		Food Grade Charcoal:
			20–25 g

		Ghee:
			4 g

		Method:

			Heat charcoal until red hot.

			Place it inside a small steel bowl.

			Keep bowl inside mayo container.

			Add ghee over charcoal.

			Immediately cover container.

		📌 NOTE:
			Smoke only for 60–75 seconds.
			Over smoking may create bitterness.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥣 Mixing Method

		Step 1:

			Whisk together:

				Thick Mayonnaise
				Fresh Cream
				Hung Curd
				Full Fat Milk

			Until completely smooth.

		---------------------------------------------------------------

		Step 2:

			Add:

				Roasted Garlic Paste
				Burnt Onion Paste

			Whisk well.

		---------------------------------------------------------------

		Step 3:

			Add all dry ingredients:

				Garlic Powder
				Roasted Cumin Powder
				Smoked Paprika
				White Pepper
				Black Pepper
				Salt
				Sugar
				MSG
				Cheddar Cheese Powder
				Butter Flavor Powder (Optional)

			Mix until smooth.

		---------------------------------------------------------------

		Step 4:

			Add:

				Melted Butter
				Neutral Oil
				Honey
				Lemon Juice
				White Vinegar

			Whisk continuously
			for 2–3 minutes.

		---------------------------------------------------------------

		Step 5:

			Give Coal Smoke
			for 60–75 seconds.

		---------------------------------------------------------------

		Step 6:

			Cover and refrigerate
			for minimum 30 minutes.

			Best Result:
				8–12 Hours Resting

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	📌 Storage

		Refrigerator:
			0–4°C

		Shelf Life:
			3–4 Days

		Always use a clean dry spoon.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Chef's Secret Tips

		✔ Use only thick premium mayonnaise.

		✔ Hung curd should be completely water-free.

		✔ Butter should be melted but not hot.

		✔ Always use freshly roasted garlic paste.

		✔ Resting overnight improves flavour by almost 30%.

		✔ Apply first layer generously on the bread.

		✔ Add a second light drizzle over the chicken before wrapping.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Final Commercial Result

		Color:
			Light Coffee Brown

		Aroma:
			Smoky
			Garlicky
			Buttery

		Taste:
			Creamy
			Rich
			Mild Sweet
			Very Mild Tang

		Texture:
			Smooth
			Silky
			Restaurant Style

		Perfect For:
			Alfam Shawarma
			Chicken Shawarma
			Chicken Rolls
			Chicken Wraps
			French Fries
			Fried Chicken Dip

🥗 Final Veg Strategy (Improved v3.0)

	Quantity: Per Shawarma

	🎯 Flavor Profile:
		Fresh
		Crunchy
		Light Sweetness
		Mild Tang
		Mild Heat
		Does Not Overpower Chicken
		Perfect Balance with Creamy Mayo

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Ingredients:

		Iceberg Lettuce (Thin Shredded): 15 g

		Pickled Onion: 12 g

		Green Capsicum (Thin Julienne): 5 g

		Pickled Jalapeño: 6 g

		Fresh Coriander (Finely Chopped): 2 g

		Fresh Mint Leaves (Thin Chiffonade): 1 g

	---------------------------------------------------------------

	Total Veg:
		41 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Ingredient Preparation

		Iceberg Lettuce

			Method:

				Wash thoroughly.

				Soak in ice water for
				10–15 minutes.

				Drain completely.

				Dry using salad spinner or cloth.

				Store refrigerated.

			📌 NOTE:
				This gives maximum crunch.

	---------------------------------------------------------------

		Pickled Onion

			Method:

				Use restaurant-style sweet pickled onion.

			📌 NOTE:
				Do not use raw onion.
				Raw onion overpowers the shawarma flavour.

	---------------------------------------------------------------

		Green Capsicum

			Method:

				Cut into very thin julienne.

			📌 NOTE:
				Thick slices reduce eating comfort.

	---------------------------------------------------------------

		Pickled Jalapeño

			Method:

				Slice thinly.

				Drain excess pickle liquid.

			📌 NOTE:
				Too much pickle liquid makes
				the wrap soggy.

	---------------------------------------------------------------

		Fresh Coriander

			Method:

				Finely chopped.

			📌 NOTE:
				Add freshness without dominating flavour.

	---------------------------------------------------------------

		Fresh Mint

			Method:

				Cut into thin chiffonade.

			📌 NOTE:
				Very small quantity only.
				It should refresh the palate,
				not taste like mint chutney.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Restaurant Secret Pickled Onion

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Red Onion: 500 g

			White Vinegar: 200 ml

			Water: 150 ml

			Sugar: 25 g

			Salt: 18 g

			Black Pepper (Crushed): 3 g

			Bay Leaf: 2 Nos.

			Mustard Seeds: 2 g

		Method:

			Slice onion thinly.

			Mix all ingredients.

			Refrigerate for
			24 Hours.

		📌 NOTE:
			Result:
				Mild Sweet
				Crunchy
				No Raw Onion Smell
				Restaurant Style

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Final Veg Layer Order

		1. Iceberg Lettuce

		2. Pickled Onion

		3. Green Capsicum

		4. Pickled Jalapeño

		5. Fresh Coriander

		6. Fresh Mint

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	📌 Storage

		Temperature:
			2–5°C

		Use Within:
			24 Hours

		Do not mix all vegetables together.

		Store each ingredient separately.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Chef's Secret Tips

		✔ Iceberg should always be ice chilled.

		✔ Drain every vegetable before use.

		✔ Never use wet vegetables.

		✔ Prepare fresh every day.

		✔ Add vegetables only after hot chicken.

		✔ Vegetables should remain cold,
		  chicken should remain hot.

		This temperature contrast gives
		the best restaurant experience.

			Perfect

🌯 Final Shawarma Assembly Order (Final v3.0)

	Quantity:
		1 Premium Alfam Shawarma

	🎯 Goal:
		Every Bite Should Contain
		Creamy Mayo
		Juicy Chicken
		Fresh Vegetables
		Perfect Balance
		No Dry Bite

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	🥄 Ingredients Per Shawarma

		Shawarma Bread (10–12"): 1 No.

		Signature Creamy Smoky Mayo: 30 g

		Juicy Alfam Chicken: 110–120 g

		Final Veg Mix: 41 g

		Chicken Glaze: 8–10 g

		Butter Brush: 5 g

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 1 – Heat the Bread

		Method:

			Heat tawa on medium flame.

			Warm bread for
			10–15 seconds each side.

		📌 NOTE:
			Bread should become soft only.
			Do not toast or make crispy.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 2 – First Mayo Layer

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Signature Creamy Smoky Mayo:
				20–22 g

		Method:

			Spread evenly over the center
			of the bread.

			Leave 2–3 cm space on all sides.

		📌 NOTE:
			This acts as the moisture barrier
			and keeps the bread soft.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 3 – Add Juicy Alfam Chicken

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Juicy Alfam Chicken:
				110–120 g

		Method:

			Arrange chicken in a straight line.

			Do not spread all over the bread.

		📌 NOTE:
			Uniform arrangement gives
			even filling in every bite.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 4 – Chicken Glaze

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Restaurant Secret Chicken Glaze:
				8–10 g

		Method:

			Drizzle evenly over hot chicken.

		📌 NOTE:
			Apply immediately after chicken.
			This locks moisture and enhances flavour.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 5 – Add Vegetables

		🥄 Layer Order:

			Iceberg Lettuce

			Pickled Onion

			Green Capsicum

			Pickled Jalapeño

		📌 NOTE:
			Keep vegetables in the center.
			Do not overfill.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 6 – Final Mayo Drizzle

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Signature Creamy Smoky Mayo:
				8–10 g

		Method:

			Drizzle in a zig-zag pattern
			over vegetables.

		📌 NOTE:
			This gives creamy flavour
			in every bite.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 7 – Wrap

		Method:

			Fold left side.

			Fold right side.

			Roll tightly from bottom.

			Seal firmly.

		📌 NOTE:
			Do not compress too tightly.
			Chicken should remain fluffy.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 8 – Butter Brush

		🥄 Ingredients:

			Melted Butter:
				5 g

		Method:

			Brush lightly over the outer surface.

		📌 NOTE:
			Brush just before toasting.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 9 – Final Toast

		Method:

			Toast on medium flame.

			45–60 seconds.

			Rotate slowly.

			Toast all sides evenly.

		📌 NOTE:
			Golden brown only.
			Do not over-toast.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	STEP 10 – Rest & Serve

		Method:

			Rest for
			30–45 seconds.

			Cut diagonally.

			Wrap in butter paper.

			Serve immediately.

		📌 NOTE:
			Resting allows mayo and juices
			to stabilize before cutting.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Chef's Secret Tips

		✔ Always keep chicken hot (60–65°C).
		✔ Always keep vegetables chilled (2–5°C).
		✔ Never place wet vegetables on bread.
		✔ Apply second mayo layer after vegetables only.
		✔ Never overfill the wrap.
		✔ Every bite should contain:
			Chicken + Mayo + Veg together.
		✔ Prepare each shawarma fresh.
		  Do not pre-assemble.

	-----------------------------------------------------------------------

	⭐ Final Commercial Result

		Outside:
			Light Crispy
		Inside:
			Juicy & Creamy
		Texture:
			Soft Chicken
			Crispy Veg
			Silky Mayo
		Flavor:
			Smoky
			Buttery
			Garlicky
			Mild Tang
			Perfectly Balanced
		Customer Experience:
			Restaurant Quality
			No Dry Bite
			No Soggy Bite
			Consistent From First Bite To Last Bite

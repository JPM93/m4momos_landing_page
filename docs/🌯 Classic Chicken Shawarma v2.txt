🌯 Classic Chicken Shawarma (Final v2.0)

Quantity: 12 Shawarmas
Chicken Quantity: 1.2 kg

Recommended Chicken: Boneless Skinless Chicken Thigh

🎯 Target Flavor Profile:
	Juicy
	Tandoori-Spiced
	Smoky
	Savoury
	Mild Tang
	Buttery Finish
	Deep Chicken Flavor
	Soft & Moist Inside

STEP 1 – First Marination:

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Lemon Juice: 25 g
		Ginger Garlic Paste: 40 g
		Salt: 14 g

	Method:
		Chicken ko large flat pieces me rakho.
		Approx. Thickness: 2–2.5 cm

		Lemon Juice, Ginger Garlic Paste aur Salt add karo.
		Chicken ko achhe se coat karo.

	📌 NOTE:
		Mix karke refrigerator me 20–30 minutes rest do.
		Chicken ko small cubes me cut mat karo.
		Large pieces Air Fryer me zyada juicy rahenge.

STEP 2 – Main Shawarma Marination:

	🥄 Creamy Base Ingredients:
		Thick Hung Curd: 150 g
		Mayonnaise: 110 g

	🥄 Fat Ingredients:
		Melted Unsalted Butter: 35 g
		Mustard Oil(Smoked & Completely Cooled): 30 g

	🥄 Main Spice Ingredients:
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 40 g
		Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 6 g
		Smoked Paprika: 4 g
		Coriander Powder: 3 g
		Cumin Powder: 2 g
		White Pepper Powder: 2 g
		MSG (Ajinomoto): 2 g
		Sugar: 5 g

	🥄 Moisture & Marinade Binding Ingredients:
		Milk Powder: 15 g
		Corn Flour: 5 g

	🥄 Aroma Ingredients:
		Garlic Paste: 15 g
		Lemon Juice: 10 g

	❌ Removed:
		Rice Flour

	📌 REASON:
		Rice Flour Air Fryer me chicken ki outer coating ko unnecessarily dry/crispy kar sakta hai.
		Hume Shawarma chicken soft aur juicy chahiye, fried-style coating nahi.

	🥣 Main Marination Method:

		Step 1:
			Hung Curd aur Mayonnaise ko smooth whisk karo.

		Step 2:
			Add Melted Butter, Smoked & Cooled Mustard Oil and Mix well.

		Step 3:
			Add Chef Tandoori Masala, Kashmiri Chilli, Smoked Paprika, Coriander Powder, Cumin Powder, White Pepper, MSG, Sugar and Mix thoroughly.

		Step 4:
			Add Milk Powder, Corn Flour and Mix until completely smooth.

		Step 5:
			Add Garlic Paste, Lemon Juice and Mix well.

		Step 6:
			STEP 1 marinated chicken add karo.
			Har chicken piece ko evenly coat karo.

	⏱ Marination Time:
		Minimum: 8 Hours
		Recommended: 12 Hours
		Best: 12–18 Hours
		Temperature: 0–4°C Refrigerator

		📌 NOTE:
			Overnight marination recommended.

STEP 3 – Air Fryer Cooking (Juicy Method):

	Preheat:
		Temperature: 180°C
		Time: 3 Minutes

	Chicken Placement:
		Chicken ko basket me single layer me arrange karo.
		Pieces ke beech thoda air-flow space rakho.
		Do NOT overcrowd.

	First Cooking:
		Temperature: 180°C
		Time: 6–7 Minutes

	Flip:
		Chicken ko flip karo.
		Very lightly brush with: Melted Butter OR Neutral Oil

	Second Cooking:
		Temperature: 180°C
		Time: Approx. 4–6 Minutes

	Temperature Check:
		Thickest chicken piece me food thermometer insert karo.
		Cook until chicken is safely cooked through.
		Do not rely only on fixed time;
		thickness and Air Fryer model can change cooking time.

	Rest: 3–5 Minutes

	📌 IMPORTANT:

		❌ 190°C Final Air Fryer Finish
		REMOVE kar diya gaya hai.
		Gas Finish already char aur colour provide karega.
		Extra high-temperature cooking chicken ko dry karegi.

STEP 4 – Quick Gas Flame Finish:

	Method:
		Air-fried chicken ko direct gas flame par finish karo.
		Approx.: 10–15 Seconds Each Side

	🎯 Goal:
		Light Char, Grilled Aroma, NOT Additional Cooking

	After Flame Brush:
		Melted Unsalted Butter: 10–12 g

	📌 NOTE:
		Chicken already cooked hai.
		Gas flame ka purpose sirf charred aroma dena hai.
		Chicken ko flame par unnecessarily hold mat karo.

STEP 5 – Coal Smoke (Dum):

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Food-Grade Charcoal: 25–30g (1 Medium Piece)
		Ghee: 4 g

	Method:
		Charcoal ko red hot karo.
		Chicken ko covered container me rakho.
		Center me small steel bowl rakho.
		Hot charcoal bowl me rakho.
		Charcoal ke upar Ghee daalo.
		Immediately cover karo.

	Smoke Time: 3–4 Minutes

	After Smoke:
		Chicken ko: 5 Minutes Rest

	📌 NOTE:
		Over-smoking mat karo.
		Chicken me already: Smoked Paprika, Mustard Oil, Gas Char present hai.
		Coal Smoke ko supporting aroma rakhna hai, dominant flavour nahi.

STEP 6 – Final Juicy Chicken Glaze:

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Concentrated Chicken Stock: 30 g
		Unsalted Butter: 12 g
		Rendered Chicken Fat: 5 g (Optional but Recommended)
		Chef Tandoori Masala: 3 g
		MSG: 0.3 g
		Lemon Juice: 3 g

	Method:
		Step 1:
			Rested chicken ko thin shawarma-style slices me cut karo.

		Step 2:
			Pan ko medium-high heat par heat karo.

		Step 3:
			Add Chicken Stock, Unsalted Butter and Rendered Chicken Fat Mix until emulsified.

		Step 4:
			Add Chef Tandoori Masala, MSG

		Step 5:
			Sliced Chicken add karo.
			Quickly toss: 20–30 Seconds Maximum

		Step 6:
			Heat OFF karo.
			Then add: 3g Lemon Juice & Quick toss.

	📌 IMPORTANT:
		Chicken ko glaze me dobara cook nahi karna.
		Sirf glaze se coat karna hai.

⭐ AIR FRYER JUICINESS RULES:
	✔ Boneless Chicken Thigh Recommended.
	✔ Chicken ko small cubes me mat kaato.
	✔ 2–2.5 cm thick pieces use karo.
	✔ Air Fryer basket overcrowd mat karo.
	✔ 1.2 kg chicken ko required ho to 2–3 batches me cook karo.
	✔ Fixed cooking time ko blindly follow mat karo.
	✔ Thermometer use karo.
	✔ Extra 190°C browning step mat karo.
	✔ Gas flame sirf quick char ke liye.
	✔ Chicken ko cooking ke baad rest zaroor do.
	✔ Rest ke baad hi slice karo.
	✔ Sliced chicken ko Chicken Stock Glaze se coat karo.
	✔ Glaze me maximum 20–30 seconds toss karo.

⭐ Final Flavor Balance:

	Total Lemon Juice:
		First Marination: 25 g
		Main Marination: 10 g
		Final Glaze: 3 g
		Total: 38 g

	📌 RESULT:
		Freshness rahegi,
		lekin chicken unnecessarily
		tangy nahi hoga.

⭐ Final Commercial Result:
	Color:
		Warm Tandoori Red
	Aroma:
		Charred, Smoky, Tandoori-Spiced

	Outside:
		Light Char, Moist Surface

	Inside:
		Soft, Juicy, Succulent

	Flavor:
		Deep Chicken, Clean Tandoori Spice, Mild Tang, Savoury Umami, Light Buttery Finish

	Perfect For:
		Classic Chicken Shawarma, Arabic Garlic Sauce, Creamy Arabic White Sauc

🧄 Signature Arabic Garlic Sauce (Final v2.0)

Quantity: ~190 g
(12 × 15 g + Handling Buffer)

🎨 Flavor Profile:
	Bold Roasted Garlic
	Creamy
	Savoury
	Mild Tang
	Light Umami
	Fresh Herby Finish
	Clean Garlic Aftertaste

🥄 Base Ingredients:
	Thick Mayonnaise: 105 g
	Hung Curd (Thick Greek Yogurt): 40 g
	Rice Bran Oil: 12 g

🥄 Garlic Ingredients:
	Roasted Garlic (Very Fine Paste): 14 g
	Fresh Parsley (Very Finely Chopped): 1.5 g

	📌 NOTE:
		Do NOT add Oregano.
		Roasted Garlic should remain the main hero flavour of this sauce.

🥄 Seasoning Ingredients:
	White Pepper Powder: 1 g
	Salt: 2 g
	Sugar: 1.5 g
	MSG: 0.5 g

🥄 Mild Tang Ingredients:
	Lemon Juice: 7 g
	White Vinegar: 1 g

🥣 Mixing Method:
	Step 1:
		Whisk together:
			Thick Mayonnaise
			Hung Curd

		Until completely smooth.

	Step 2:
		Add:
			Roasted Garlic Paste
		Whisk until completely incorporated.

	Step 3:
		Rice Bran Oil ko slowly add
		karte hue continuously whisk karo.

	Step 4:
		Add:
			Lemon Juice
			White Vinegar
		& Mix well.

	Step 5:
		Add:
			White Pepper
			Salt
			Sugar
			MSG
		Whisk until completely smooth.

	Step 6:
		Add:
			Fresh Parsley: 1.5 g
		Gently fold into the sauce.
		Do not over-blend after adding parsley.

	Step 7:
		Whisk for another 1–2 minutes.
		Cover and refrigerate.
		Minimum Rest: 3–4 Hours
		Best Result: 6–8 Hours Rest

📌 Storage:
	Refrigerator:
		0–4°C

	Best Quality:
		Use as fresh as possible.

	📌 NOTE:
		Fresh parsley introduces additional moisture and handling risk, so strict refrigeration and clean utensils are important.

⭐ Chef's Secret Tips:
	✔ Garlic ko completely burn mat karo.
	✔ Golden roasted garlic best rahega.
	✔ Garlic paste completely smooth honi chahiye.
	✔ Fresh parsley extremely finely chop karo.
	✔ Oregano add mat karo.
	✔ Butter Essence remove kar diya gaya hai.
	✔ Sauce ka main flavour hamesha
	✔ Roasted Garlic hi rehna chahiye.

⭐ Final Result:
	Primary Taste: Bold Roasted Garlic
	Secondary Taste: Creamy + Savoury
	Herbal Note: Light Fresh Parsley
	Tanginess: Mild
	Sweetness: Very Low
	Texture: Thick, Smooth, Creamy
	Role In Shawarma: Bold Garlic Sauce

🤍 Signature Creamy Arabic White Sauce (Final v2.0)

Quantity: ~200 g
(12 × 15 g + Handling Buffer)

🎨 Flavor Profile:
	Extra Creamy
	Milky
	Silky
	Mild Cheesy
	Light Buttery
	Mild Sweetness
	Very Mild Garlic
	Light Herby Aroma
	Almost No Tang
	Cooling Cream Sauce

🥄 Creamy Base Ingredients:
	Thick Mayonnaise: 90 g
	Fresh Cream: 45 g
	Hung Curd (Thick): 25 g
	Full Fat Milk: 15 g

🥄 Richness Ingredients:
	Unsalted Butter (Melted & Cooled): 8 g
	Cheddar Cheese Powder: 3 g

🥄 Flavor Ingredients:
	Garlic Powder: 0.5 g
	White Pepper Powder: 0.7 g
	Dried Oregano: 0.5 g
	Dried Parsley: 0.5 g
	Honey: 4 g
	Sugar: 1 g
	Salt: 1.5 g
	MSG: 0.3 g

🥄 Very Mild Tang:

	Lemon Juice: 2 g
	📌 NOTE:
		Do NOT add White Vinegar.

		This sauce should remain creamy,
		milky and only very mildly acidic.

🥣 Mixing Method:
	Step 1:
		Whisk together:
			Thick Mayonnaise, Fresh Cream, Hung Curd, Full Fat Milk
		Until completely smooth and silky.

	Step 2:
		Add Cheddar Cheese Powder, Garlic Powder, White Pepper, Salt, Sugar, MSG and Whisk well.

	Step 3:
		Add Dried Oregano & Dried Parsley and Mix gently.

	Step 4:
		Add Honey & Lemon Juice and Mix until completely incorporated.

	Step 5:
		Slowly add: Melted Unsalted Butter: 8 g

		📌 Butter should be melted but NOT hot.

		Whisk continuously for 2–3 minutes.

	Step 6: Cover and refrigerate.
		Minimum Rest: 3–4 Hours
		Best Result: 6–8 Hours Rest

	📌 NOTE:
		Resting is important because dried Oregano and Parsley need time to hydrate and release their flavour.

📌 Storage:
	Refrigerator:
		0–4°C

	Best Quality:
		Use as fresh as possible.

	Always use a clean dry spoon.

⭐ Do NOT Add:
	Fresh Garlic
	Roasted Garlic
	White Vinegar
	Smoked Paprika
	Coal Smoke
	Butter Essence

	📌 NOTE:
		These ingredients will push this sauce towards Garlic Sauce or Alfam Mayo profile.

⭐ Chef's Secret Tips:
	✔ Use Full Fat Milk.
	✔ Use real Unsalted Butter.
	✔ Butter should be melted and slightly cooled.
	✔ Do not increase Garlic Powder unnecessarily.
	✔ Oregano should remain a background aroma, not taste like pizza seasoning.
	✔ Do not increase Cheese Powder above 3g without testing.
	✔ Sauce should remain white/cream in colour.

⭐ Final Result:
	Primary Taste: Creamy + Milky
	Secondary Taste: Mild Cheesy + Buttery
	Herbal Note: Oregano + Parsley
	Garlic: Very Mild Background Note
	Sweetness: Mild
	Tanginess: Very Low
	Texture: Thick, Silky, Smooth
	Role In Shawarma: Cooling Creamy White Sauce

🥗 Final Veg Strategy – Classic Chicken Shawarma (Final v2.0)

Quantity: Per Shawarma

🎯 Flavor Profile:
	Fresh
	Cold & Crunchy
	Light Tang
	Clean
	Refreshing
	Does Not Overpower Chicken
	Balances Garlic & Creamy White Sauce

🥄 Ingredients:
	Iceberg Lettuce (Thin Shredded): 20 g
	Pickled Onion: 12 g
	Cucumber (Thin Julienne): 8 g

Total Veg: 40 g

⭐ Ingredient Preparation

	Iceberg Lettuce:
		Method:
			Wash thoroughly.
			Soak in Ice-Cold Water: 10–15 Minutes
			Drain completely.
			Dry properly using: Salad Spinner OR Clean Kitchen Cloth Thinly shred.
			Keep refrigerated until service.

		📌 NOTE:
			Iceberg should be: Cold, Crispy & Completely Dry
			Wet lettuce shawarma bread ko soggy kar sakti hai.

	Pickled Onion:
		Quantity: 12 g

		Method:
			Use Restaurant-Style Pickled Red Onion.
			Before adding to shawarma, excess pickling liquid completely drain karo.

		📌 NOTE:
			Do NOT add raw onion.
			Pickled Onion provides:
				Crunch
				Mild Acidity
				Light Sweetness
				Reduced Raw Onion Smell

			12 g se zyada unnecessary mat karo because Garlic Sauce already strong flavour provide karega.

	Cucumber:

		Quantity: 8 g

		Method:
			Use Fresh Firm Cucumber.
			Cucumber ko lengthwise cut karo.
			Agar center bahut watery / seedy ho:
				Soft Seed Core Remove Karo.
			Outer firm cucumber ko thin julienne me cut karo.
			Cut karne ke baad paper towel / clean cloth se surface moisture remove karo.
			Keep refrigerated.

		📌 NOTE:
			Cucumber ka purpose:
				Freshness, Cooling Effect & Light Crunch
			Water add karna nahi.
			Isliye cucumber ko completely dry use karo.

⭐ Final Veg Ratio Per Shawarma:
	Iceberg Lettuce: 20g 50%
	Pickled Onion: 12g 30%
	Cucumber: 8g 20%
	Total: 40g 100%

⭐ Final Veg Layer Order:
	1. Iceberg Lettuce
	2. Pickled Onion
	3. Cucumber Julienne

📌 Important – Do NOT Premix
	Iceberg Lettuce, Pickled Onion & Cucumber
	ko service se pehle ek container me mix mat karo.
	Store Separately.

	Reason:
		Pickled Onion liquid release karega.
		Cucumber moisture release karega.
		Iceberg soft ho jayega.
		Crunch reduce hoga.

🧊 Storage & Service:
	Vegetables: Keep Refrigerated
	Prepare: Preferably Fresh Daily
	Pickled Onion: Separate Airtight Container
	Cucumber: Separate Container
	Iceberg: Separate Dry Container

	📌 NOTE:
		Cold Veg + Hot Chicken ka temperature contrast shawarma ko fresh mouthfeel deta hai.

⭐ Do NOT Add:
	❌ Fresh Coriander
	❌ Fresh Mint
	❌ Green Capsicum
	❌ Jalapeño
	❌ Tomato
	❌ Cabbage

	📌 REASON:
		Classic Chicken Shawarma me already:
			Tandoori-Spiced Chicken
			Arabic Garlic Sauce
			Creamy Herby White Sauce
			Pickled Onion
		hain.

		Extra vegetables/herbs add karne se
		flavour unnecessarily busy ho jayega.

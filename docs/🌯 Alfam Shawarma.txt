🔥 Signature Smoky Coffee-Tone Tandoori Mayo

	Quantity: ~380 g (30 g × 12 = 360 g + handling buffer)

	🎨 Flavor Profile:
		Light Coffee-Brown Color
		Smoky
		Creamy
		Roasted Onion Depth
		Mild Tang
		Tandoori Aroma
		Buttery Finish Compatible

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Thick Mayonnaise: 240 g
		Fresh Cream: 20 g
		Hung Curd (Thick): 45 g
		Mustard Oil (heated & cooled): 8 g
		Chef Tandoori Masala(DIY Made): 8 g
		Roasted Cumin Powder: 4 g
		Smoked Paprika: 5 g
		Garlic Powder: 3 g
		Ginger Powder: 1.5 g
		Black Pepper: 2 g
		Lemon Juice: 15 g
		Honey: 6 g
		Light Soy Sauce: 4 g
		White Vinegar: 4 g
		Salt: 2.5 g
		MSG: 1 g
		Burnt Onion Paste: 22 g

	⭐ Secret Ingredient 1 – Burnt Onion Paste
		Medium Onion: ~100-120g raw
		
		Method:
			Onion ko beech se 2 hisson me kaat lo.
			Direct flame par 2–3 minute rotate karte hue roast karo.
			Outer burnt layer hata do.
			Soft roasted onion ko blend karke smooth paste bana lo.
			Sauce me 22 g use karo.
			NOTE: Ye sauce ko naturally light coffee-brown color aur caramelized roasted depth deta hai.
	
	⭐ Secret Ingredient 2 – Coal Smoke
		Charcoal: 20-25 g
		Ghee: 4 g
		
		Method:
			Charcoal ko red hot karo.
			Steel ki chhoti katori me rakho.
			Sauce ke bowl ke beech me katori rakho.
			Coal par 4 g ghee daalo.
			Bowl ko turant cover karo.
			📌 NOTE: Maximum some time 60-75 seconds

	🥣 Mixing Method
		Step 1:
			Whisk together: Mayonnaise, Hung Curd & Fresh Cream.
		Step 2:
			Add Burnt Onion Paste & Whisk
		Step 3:
			Add all dry spices(Chef Tandoori Masala, Roasted Cumin, Smoked Paprika, Garlic Powder, Ginger Powder, Black Pepper, Salt, MSG)
			Mix until smooth.
		Step 4:
			Add all Lemon Joice, Soy Sauce, Vinegar, Honey, Musard Oil & Whisk for 2-3 minutes.
		Step 5:
			Give Coal Smoke for 60-75 seconds.
		Step 6:
			Refrigerate for 30 minutes before use.
	
	📌 Note: 3-4/days @0–4°C in refrigerator

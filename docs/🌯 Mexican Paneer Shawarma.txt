🌯 Mexican Paneer Shawarma

	Quantity: 12 shawarmas
	🧀Paneer Quantity: 1.2 kg (12 × 100 g)
	🧀 Cutting size 20mm cubes.

		Creamy

			Lemon Juice: 12 g

		🥄 Spice Blend Ingredients:

				Skin removal.
				Seeds removed.

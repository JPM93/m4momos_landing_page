🔥 Signature Fire Harissa Style Shawarma Paste

	Quantity: ~250 g (4-10 g for 30-40 shawarmas)

	🥄 Ingredients:
		Tomato Paste: 100 g
		Roasted Red Bell Pepper Puree: 40 g
		Garlic Paste: 15 g
		Kashmiri Chilli Powder: 8 g
		Hot Red Chilli Powder: 3 g
		Crushed Chilli Flakes: 3 g
		Smoked Paprika: 5 g
		Roasted Cumin Powder: 3 g
		Coriander Powder: 2 g
		Black Pepper: 1 g
		Oregano (crushed): 1 g
		White Vinegar: 8 g
		Lemon Juice: 10 g
		Honey: 5 g
		Worcestershire Sauce: 3 g
		Light Soy Sauce: 3 g
		Salt: 3 g
		Sugar: 2 g
		Sunflower Oil: 20 g
		Water(as needed): 15–25 g

	Cooking Method:
		Step 1:
			Pan me 20 g oil Medium flame.

		Step 2:
			Garlic paste 15g 40–50 second bhuno.
			Brown nahi karna.

		Step 3:
			Roasted Bell Pepper Puree 40g Add karo.
			1 minute cook.

		Step 4:
			Tomato Paste 100g add.
			4 minute roast.
			Oil halka separate hone lage.

		Step 5:
			Saare powder spices add.
			30 second.

		Step 6:
			Add, Vinegar, Lemon, Honey, Worcestershire, Soy & Mix all.

		Step 7:
			Water add. 15–25 g
			Sirf smooth spread consistency tak.

		Step 8:
			2 minute simmer. Gas band.

		Step 9: (SECRET)
			Cool hone ke baad Coal Smoke(only for 45/seconds)
			15 g Charcoal & 3 g Ghee

		Final Rest:
			Fridge for 12 hours next day use.
			Ye step mat skip karna.

		Per Shawarma:
			Mild: 0g, Medium: 5g, Spicy: 8g, Fire: 10-12g
